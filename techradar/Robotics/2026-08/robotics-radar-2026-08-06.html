Personalized physical therapy: Stroke rehabilitation powered by AI

Researchers at MIT have developed an adaptive physical therapy system that uses generative AI to learn from physical therapists and interactively support stroke patients, teaching robots how to assist with physical and occupational therapy without replacing human care. The system combines transformer-based diffusion models with real-time force feedback, enabling the robot to safely adjust assistance based on each patient's capabilities. The approach has been tested in a clinical study and has potential applications beyond physical therapy, such as in industrial settings or collaborative workspaces.

arXiv - cs.RO

Kitchen Robotic Manipulation utilizing Foundation Models

A modular perception pipeline is presented for household manipulation tasks, focusing on dishware handling in kitchen environments. The pipeline integrates object detection, multi-view segmentation, 3D reconstruction, and feature fusion for 6D pose estimation and grasp planning. A custom kitchen dataset is used to evaluate the best-performing configuration, achieving an ADI of 89.12%. This configuration enables successful execution of tasks on physical robots without environment-specific retraining.

arXiv - cs.RO

SiMDex: Mining Similar Egocentric Videos for Cross-Embodiment Dexterous Manipulation

Researchers Nie Lin and colleagues developed SiMDex, a framework to select optimal human videos for training robot manipulation tasks by mining similar egocentric videos. The framework uses a three-layer pipeline to extract task-relevant subsets from a pool of 32M human samples, achieving an overall success rate improvement from 47.7% to 61.1%. SiMDex outperforms indiscriminate data mixing using only 1.49M mined samples (<5% of the pool).

arXiv - cs.RO

Feasibility of Embedded Photoplethysmography Sensing in Short-Duration Tactile Interactions With Pocket-Sized Robots Using IMU- and Confidence-Based Filtering

Researchers developed and tested an embedded photoplethysmography (PPG) sensor in a pocket-sized robot to enable continuous heart rate monitoring during tactile interactions. The sensor was paired with an Inertial Measurement Unit (IMU) for motion artifact rejection, and a confidence-based smoothing algorithm for recovery periods. The filtering strategy significantly reduced Mean Absolute Percentage Error and achieved statistical equivalence to ground truth measurements. However, the sensor required stability over longer periods to converge, limiting its effectiveness in short-duration interactions.

Approximate Multi-Objective Search Under Rulebooks

Omar Muhammetkulyyev, Oren Salzman, and Tichakorn Wongpiromsarn introduce epsilon-rule-dominance, a notion of approximate dominance under rulebooks, to address the computational challenge of computing full set of rulebook-optimal solutions. arXiv - cs.RO

SCOPE: Field-of-View-Aware Path Planning in Unknown 3D Environments via Safety-Volume Certification

SCOPE (Safety Certification through Observation Planning and Execution) is a path planning framework that decouples optimistic goal-directed guidance from certified execution in unknown 3D environments. It uses online safety-volume certification to ensure the complete robot-inflated volume of an intended motion can be observed and verified free before execution. The system converts uncertain points along optimistic routes into explicit observation obligations, resolves them through target-centric viewpoint search, and recursively clears intermediate obligations when useful viewpoints are not yet certified-reachable. SCOPE reaches 100% success rate in 60 randomized tasks while maintaining near-zero entry into non-certified inflated space.

arXiv - cs.RO

SSC: A Verifiable Structured Representation for Bimanual Manipulation Labelling

SSC (Structured Subtask Chain) proposes a state-transition representation to bridge the extremes between natural language descriptions and rigid template formats. Each Structured Subtask Template (SST) stores core action components, flexible conditions, base-motion field, and after-state scene graph. SSC supports three vision-language assisted functions: rendering SSTs as natural language, checking against four state-transition rules, and completing underspecified fields through a query resolution cascade. The pipeline is instantiated on BEHAVIOR-1K for logic verification and content completion.
