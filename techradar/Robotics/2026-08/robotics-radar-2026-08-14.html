Northrop Grumman launches robotic spacecraft to repair and extend life of satellites

Northrop Grumman has launched the Mission Robotic Vehicle (MRV), a robotic spacecraft designed to service satellites in orbit, capable of satellite inspection, relocation, repair, upgrades, debris removal, and robotic assembly. The MRV features two fully articulated robotic arms that can manipulate specialized tools while conducting servicing operations. It will be able to install propulsion modules on existing satellites, extending their life without replacing the entire satellite. The spacecraft itself has a passive refueling module and computing hardware intended to support future AI-powered operations.

Schaeffler unveils formed strain wave gearboxes for humanoid robots

Schaeffler is expanding its humanoid robotics product portfolio with formed strain wave gearboxes, key components in actuators that make up around half of the total costs of manufacturing a humanoid. The company's innovative forming technology can manufacture these components in seconds, cutting production time by minutes and reducing material consumption by over 75%. This method achieves comparable torques and efficiencies to conventionally manufactured components while offering high dimensional accuracy and process stability. Production will start in Germany in 2027 with plans to roll out to other regions.

Sony robot hand patent hints at renewed interest in humanoid robotics

Sony has filed a US patent application for a robotic hand featuring a human-like opposable thumb, providing evidence of the electronics giant's renewed research into humanoid and embodied robotics. The application, published by the US Patent and Trademark Office on August 6, describes a robot hand designed to reproduce the movements that make the human thumb important for manipulating objects. The patent focuses on the mechanical aspect of the hand mechanism, which breaks movement into three separate rotation points to enable opposition between the thumb and fingers. This design could have applications beyond humanoid robots, including service robots, remotely operated machines, and systems using tools designed for humans.

RoboSynChallenge: Mastering Real-World Dexterity via Generalizing Synthesized Manipulation Skills

RoboSynChallenge: Mastering Real-World Dexterity via Generalizing Synthesized Manipulation Skills introduces a unified benchmark for evaluating and advancing robotic manipulation policies across various tasks, environments, and difficulty levels. The challenge integrates large-scale synthetic data generation with standardized real-world robotic evaluation to foster the development of broadly capable, data-efficient, and adaptable manipulation systems. Participants are encouraged to leverage synthesized state-action trials to improve general-purpose policy learning, while final assessments are conducted exclusively on unseen real-world manipulation environments.

Researchers have developed Attune, a self-annotation tool to understand robot operator attention profiles. FUSE: Active Functional Affordance Grounding through Adaptive Semantic-Geometric Evidence Acquisition

FUSE, an adaptive semantic-geometric evidence acquisition framework, combines explicit uncertainty-driven exploration with a learned amortized planner to efficiently select informative viewpoints for embodied agents. This approach enables active functional affordance grounding, where an agent sequentially explores a scene to identify and spatially ground an object satisfying a functional query. FUSE achieves the highest observed non-oracle grounding performance while reducing computation by 1.33x relative to fully explicit exploration.

SAP-Nav: Spatial Semantic Representation Meets Active Perception for Hierarchical Open-Vocabulary Object Navigation

SAP-Nav, a fully online and zero-shot framework, addresses the challenge of hierarchical open-vocabulary object navigation (OVON) through active perception. It constructs a Queryable Spatial-Semantic Representation from actively acquired room views, enabling spatial semantic queries from any explored location. SAP-Nav employs Active Viewpoint Verification to assess observation evidence and reposition the agent for more informative viewpoints before verifying candidates against category and attribute constraints.

This paper proposes a decentralized approach using a genetic fuzzy system to coordinate multiple robots in planetary missions. The system minimizes the total path length while avoiding obstacles by analyzing an elevation map and converting it into a traversability map. A genetic algorithm optimizes fuzzy inference systems for transporting objects, which are then validated in different testing environments.

AirForesight: Current-to-Future Spatial Map Imagination with Cross-Space Planning Consistency for UAV-VLN

AirForesight, a current-to-future spatial map imagination framework for UAV-VLN, learns a structured current-map representation from multi-view observations and jointly supervises it with future-trajectory prediction. This framework uses structured causal attention to propagate current spatial knowledge to future-map reasoning, resulting in predicted next 3D waypoints. A cross-space planning consistency loss is introduced to encourage directional agreement between the predicted map-space trajectory and expert action direction. Experiments demonstrate strong performance on OpenUAV and AerialVLN-S datasets with extensive ablations.

BrainWAM: Action-Space Coordination of Semantic Priors and Predictive Dynamics for Autonomous Driving

BrainWAM, a structured action-space coordination framework, combines semantic reasoning and predictive world modeling into two specialized pathways. It uses compact action representations to align these pathways and introduces an asynchronous rectified-flow inference strategy with decoupled video and action denoising. BrainWAM reaches state-of-the-art performance on both NAVSIM v1 and NAVSIM v2, outperforming VLA-only or WAM-only methods.

AMR-Pose: An Active LED Marker-Based Relative Pose Estimation Framework With Probabilistic Switching PnP for Cooperative AUVs

AMR-Pose, an active LED marker-based relative pose estimation framework, was developed to improve robust vision-based relative localization in underwater environments. The framework combines Lie-group pose propagation, probabilistic marker association, and visibility-adaptive measurement fusion for accurate six-degree-of-freedom relative pose estimation. It adapts the estimation process according to marker visibility, maintaining geometric consistency and temporal stability during partial observations and visibility transitions.
