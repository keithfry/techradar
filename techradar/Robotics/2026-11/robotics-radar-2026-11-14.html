Adapt Human Motion for Robots with NVIDIA SOMA Retargeter

NVIDIA SOMA Retargeter adapts human motion data to humanoid robots by using the shared SOMA skeleton representation. It scales motion targets, solves inverse kinematics using NVIDIA Warp, and applies post-processing like foot-plant adjustment to fit robot proportions. Developers can source motion from various datasets or generative models and export retargeted trajectories for policy training workflows. Tools like the Robot Configurator allow for refining robot configurations and optimizing inverse-kinematics priorities for smoother motion. The system enables the adaptation of human movement to teach humanoid robots locomotion and for simulation evaluation.

How Rhoda AI is using video data to create generalized AI models for robots

Rhoda AI pretrains models on internet-scale video to build a strong understanding of motion, physics, and physical interaction for robotics. The company's Direct Video Action (DVA) model combines video history and language tokens to predict future video frames and convert those predictions into robot actions. This process involves an observation, prediction, action execution, and a new observation loop for continuous learning. Using broad video data allows the models to generalize across different robot forms, and post-training with task-specific data refines the skills for real-world applications. Rhoda AI aims to use this generalized approach to advance robotics in manufacturing and logistics.

Why Build a Humanoid Robot From the Sensor Up? The Thinking Behind GENTO’s CoRL 2026 Debut

GENTO's humanoid robot development stems from incorporating MEMS torque sensors within each joint, which dictate robot reactions to contact. Tianji Intelligence developed these sensors and built a five-tier system: sensors, integrated joint modules, dual arms, whole-body systems, and a dexterous hand. The system utilizes high-sensitivity sensors capable of measuring torque as low as 0.02 N·m for accurate force control. This approach enabled demonstrations, such as the TIANJI Marvin Pro platform, used in NVIDIA's GR00T 2 open-world action model. The company's philosophy centers on achieving gentle interaction by starting with precise joint torque readings.

Apple HALO reaches reported 91% success on Unitree G1 tests

The research sought to determine if synthetic experience transfers effectively to unfamiliar physical spaces.
