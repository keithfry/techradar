DP World partners with Dubai Future Foundation to develop autonomous port logistics

Dubai Future Foundation partnered with DP World to become the first strategic partner for SHIFFT, a self-driving logistics joint venture. This collaboration aims to develop and deploy autonomous logistics solutions across ports, terminals, and industrial zones. The project will focus on vehicle integration, autonomous driving, fleet management, and operational optimization at locations like Jebel Ali Port. SHIFFT seeks to create integrated solutions that operate within DP World's technology environments globally. This initiative intends to enhance supply chain resilience and boost Dubai's standing in smart logistics.

AGV Expo attracts more than 1,000 visitors as industrial automation gathers pace

The inaugural AGV Expo in Piacenza, Italy, attracted over 1,000 visitors, showcasing advancements in automated material handling and mobile robotics. The event featured new products like the compact PROXAUT UNDER-X AGV and various autonomous handling robots. A key theme was the necessity of system integration, as successful automation depends on adapting robots to existing factory conditions and infrastructure. Discussions also covered essential aspects like cybersecurity, regulations, and return on investment for mobile robotics. The expo is scheduled to return in 2027 in Parma, Italy, with expectations for increased attendance.

Embodied AI unveils AIR humanoid robot designed to learn factory tasks on the job

Embodied AI unveiled AIR, a humanoid robot designed to learn factory tasks by working alongside people. AIR is built to perform repetitive, high-dexterity tasks safely, utilizing a tendon-driven architecture and running on wheels. The robot learns by observing human demonstrations, with an operator overseeing the initial tasks to ensure safety and reliability. This approach allows the robot to acquire skills over time, ensuring that errors have no immediate physical consequences. The company emphasizes safety and reliability as non-negotiable principles in physical AI development.

University of Rhode Island showcases underwater robotics and autonomous systems

The NOAA Ocean Exploration Cooperative Institute hosted an underwater systems and autonomy showcase in October featuring technology companies Sonardyne and Voyis alongside University of Rhode Island researchers. The event focused on advancements in subsea technology for ocean exploration and monitoring. Demonstrations included using Sonardyne and Voyis systems for deep ocean current observation and high-resolution shipwrecks documentation. Researchers also showcased autonomous platforms like ALBEX and REMUS underwater vehicles. The showcase facilitated collaboration between industry and academia to address challenges in underwater science and technology.

Neura and E.ON partner to explore humanoids for power grids

Neura Robotics, E.ON, and enviaM have partnered to develop robots for electricity network operations, with field trials scheduled for 2027. The project will focus on using humanoids, such as the 4NE1, for infrastructure inspection, monitoring, and support of workers in physically demanding tasks. The robots are designed to navigate existing facilities like stairs and control cabinets without major structural changes. Training will occur at TUM RoboGym using Neura's multimodal AI model and motion capture techniques. The pilot partner, Mitnetz Strom, is expected to gain practical experience with the technology in 2027.
