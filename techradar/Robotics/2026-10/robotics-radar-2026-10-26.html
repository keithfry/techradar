The Air Behind the Robot: Why Compressed Air Piping Decides How Well an Automated Line Runs

Compressed air dictates the performance of automated lines because the quality and pressure of the supply directly affect the operation of pneumatic components. Inadequate air delivery causes cycle times to stretch and parts to slip, often leading to maintenance issues that are hard to trace. Leaks, corrosion from moisture, and material choices in the piping network significantly impact long-term operational reliability. Effective distribution requires careful leak detection and material selection to minimize production losses. Modern designs favor flexible loop systems and quick-connect fittings to accommodate plant changes while maintaining system integrity.

ETH Zurich team demonstrates Unitree G1 climbing and crawling

An ETH Zurich team developed a single policy enabling a Unitree G1 to walk, climb, and crawl without manually scripting transitions between these movements. They trained the system by using 13.2 minutes of human motion data to learn contact patterns. Reinforcement learning in simulation was then used to allow the robot to adapt to new terrains. During operation, onboard sensing and a goal guide the robot's movements without requiring a reference motion for transitions. The team demonstrated climbing, including a ladder recovery, using the learned policy rather than explicitly programmed sequences.

See What I Need, Dream What I Want: A Navigation Foundation Model on a Self-Constructed Representation Space

RepresentNav is a navigation foundation model that constructs a self-constructed representation space from vision foundation models. It uses Representation Residuals, a transformer, to aggregate features from multiple vision models to understand navigation needs. The model also generates "dreams" through a latent chain-of-thought to predict scene representations for future moments. This dream generation is trained jointly with the navigation policy via a joint-embedding predictive architecture (JEPA). RepresentNav achieves state-of-the-art results on several benchmarks and improves physical robot performance amid clutter.

KiRoSe: Using Kinematic Data for Efficient Segmentation of Robot Demonstrations

KiRoSe is a framework that utilizes robot kinematic data for efficiently segmenting robot demonstrations. It proposes Sparse Motion Tokens (SMTs), a discrete vocabulary to compactly represent recurring kinematic events, inspired by entropy coding. SMTs are learned by encoding trajectories with temporal supervision and quantizing features. Evaluation shows KiRoSe significantly outperforms baseline methods in F1 quality segmentation. Furthermore, the method can track task progress and detect action reversals on unseen robots.

arXiv - cs.RO

SEVI: Safe Evidential Real-World Reinforcement Learning

SEVI is a framework designed for reliability-aware safety intervention during real-world reinforcement learning to address unsafe interactions. It introduces an Evidential Safety Critic that estimates future constraint-violation risk and epistemic uncertainty in a single forward pass. During online learning, SEVI checks actions before execution and overrides them with an offline-trained recovery policy if risk is too high. The method was evaluated on simulated and real-world manipulation tasks, demonstrating superior safety prediction and reduced unsafe events compared to other methods. SEVI successfully reduces unsafe events by 13.4 to 57.1 percent while maintaining low-latency execution.

WorldMirror: Agentic Real-to-Sim Generation of Interactive Worlds from In-the-Wild Robot Demonstrations

WorldMirror is an agentic framework that generates interactive digital twins from in-the-wild robot demonstrations. It treats real-to-sim construction as iteratively refining an executable world program through agent planning and tool orchestration. The system utilizes a camera-pose-anchored scene representation to organize spatial context, geometry, and interactive assets. An agent refines reconstructions by diagnosing and correcting errors to ensure physically consistent interactions and trajectory replay. Experiments show that WorldMirror can create visually faithful and geometrically consistent interactive worlds while maintaining the physical validity of recorded robot trajectories.

FORCA: A Force-Aware Coding Agent for Contact-Rich Robot Manipulation

FORCA is a coding agent designed for contact-rich robot manipulation that translates visual observations and force/torque measurements into Python programs. It inspects force records and visual outcomes between executions to diagnose failures and revise control logic. The agent achieves this without requiring demonstrations, task-specific training, or simulation. Tested across five real-robot tasks, FORCA improves manipulation accuracy and adapts to changes in object properties. Compared to a vision-only agent, FORCA enhances grasp accuracy and reduces contact loss during tasks like wiping and peeling.

arXiv - cs.RO

EgoForesight: Effectively Scaling Policy Learning with Noisy Egocentric Actions

EgoForesight is a vision-language-action framework designed to scale policy learning despite noisy egocentric actions. It learns jointly from robot demonstrations and egocentric videos by mitigating the influence of unreliable action labels. The method employs a joint vision-action flow and reliability-conditioned corruption to manage noisy supervision. Furthermore, it utilizes privileged-future representation distillation from demonstrated outcomes to provide complementary supervision. This approach results in steeper scaling slopes, improving performance in real-world and simulated dexterous manipulation tasks.

Multimodal MPC via Bayesian Set Optimization for Dynamic Manipulation of Deformable Linear Objects

The paper introduces BM2PC, a Bayesian Multimodal MPC framework for the agile manipulation of deformable linear objects (DLOs). It formulates multimodal planning as a set optimization problem to balance control quality and behavioral diversity. BM2PC efficiently updates multiple promising control modes with low computational overhead through an incremental approach. The framework incorporates an execution dynamics model to compensate for real-world execution mismatches during manipulation. Experiments show BM2PC achieves accurate long-horizon tracking and efficient obstacle avoidance in simulation and hardware.
