Planet ROS

ROS Discourse General: MOLA cloud: free online LiDAR SLAM from ROS bags, plus camera and LiDAR-camera calibration

MOLA cloud is a free web service that allows users to perform LiDAR SLAM and camera calibration directly in the browser using ROS 2 data. Users can upload ROS 2 bags to download 3D maps and input videos or image sets to derive camera intrinsics. The service automates the process of extracting point cloud maps, trajectory data, and extrinsic LiDAR-camera calibration. It supports various input formats and provides tools for viewing and further processing the resulting maps. The service operates on secure, isolated servers within the EU and enforces monthly usage limits.

Figure AI patents wearable system for teaching humanoid robots through human movement

Figure AI patented a wearable robotic system to capture human arm movements for training or remotely controlling humanoid robots. The system uses a backpack frame with articulated arms equipped with sensors to measure movements at the shoulder, elbow, and wrist. A Dozen Completely DIY Robot Actuators

Koshiro Robot created a dozen custom robot actuator motors, including their drivers and Field-Oriented Control (FOC) firmware. These actuators were designed for easy assembly and mounting, featuring a 3D-printed gearbox and motor controller outside the stator for better cooling and wiring simplicity. The design incorporates CAN-bus-plus-XT-60 daisy chaining for power and signal distribution. This approach multiplies cost savings by creating multiple units. The project demonstrates an experimentalist-friendly approach to reducing the cost of robot components.
