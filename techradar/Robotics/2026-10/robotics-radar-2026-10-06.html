Agility CEO Peggy Johnson Joins Project Meridian, Bringing Humanoid Logistics Expertise to Defense Study

Agility Robotics CEO Peggy Johnson will join Project Meridian, a MITRE-led study on future warfare, as an individual consultant. She will contribute expertise from commercial humanoid deployments to discussions about military logistics and operational support. Johnson suggests humanoids can assist service personnel by handling demanding, repetitive logistics tasks to improve supply chains. Project Meridian aims to develop recommendations over a long-term horizon for future warfighting capabilities. This participation places commercial humanoid experience within a strategic defense planning context.

ROS Discourse General: University of Waterloo user study on understanding CMake changes

A user study is being conducted by a University of Waterloo Ph.D. candidate to understand how developers perceive changes to CMake build specifications. The study evaluates a new approach designed to help users identify the impact of these modifications on the resulting build configuration. Participation is open to individuals aged 18–64 with any experience in CMake, programming, or code review. The study is entirely online, lasts about one hour, and will not collect participants' names. The research has received clearance from the University of Waterloo Research Ethics Board.

Meta Job Listing Explicitly References Its Humanoid Robot Hardware

A Meta job listing for a Mechanical Design Engineer explicitly references work on humanoid robot hardware after its initial release. The role focuses on maintaining and improving the physical machines through addressing mechanical failures and assembly improvements. Responsibilities include investigating component issues, implementing design changes, and working with fabrication teams to resolve build problems. The position requires experience in manufacturing and sustaining engineering, involving components like motors and actuators. This posting details the engineering work required to support the development and production of Meta's humanoid robot hardware.
