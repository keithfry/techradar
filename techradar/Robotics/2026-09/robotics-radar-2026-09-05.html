Data from drones in Ukraine is fueling a new Wild West marketplace | MIT Technology Review

Drone data from Ukraine is being converted into a resource for the defense sector and commercial companies, creating a new marketplace. This data, generated from chaotic battlefield conditions, is highly valuable for training AI models by capturing real-world operational experience. This process transforms combat into a commercial asset, allowing for the development of AI that benefits both military and civilian applications. Concerns exist regarding the lack of regulation over this data, the consent of those in the footage, and the potential for an extractive economy. Therefore, there is a need for a regulatory system to govern the flow of battlefield data from combat to commercial products.

AGIBOT Deepens European Push at IFA 2026 with TÜV Rheinland Certification and Tekpoint Retail Alliance

AGIBOT showcased its multi-robot portfolio, including the AGIBOT A3 humanoid, at IFA 2026 in Berlin. The company secured crucial regulatory compliance certifications, including RED DA, RED NB, and Safety AK from TÜV Rheinland. AGIBOT formed a commercial alliance with Tekpoint to facilitate regional retail and service robotics distribution across Europe. This strategy combines localized hardware assembly in Serbia with continental market integration in Western Europe. The overall goal is to expand AGIBOT's commercial presence by addressing both technical compliance and regional market access.

Tuya Unveils Doova: An AI Companion Robot Targeting Elder Care and Smart Home Hubs

Tuya Smart introduced Doova, an autonomous companion robot for independently living seniors, at IFA 2026. It uses LDS LiDAR, microphones, and computer vision for proactive fall detection and emergency escalation. Doova incorporates a multimodal large language model for conversational support, reading documents, and managing wellness schedules. The robot functions as a mobile smart home hub, controlling connected appliances via voice commands. Although promising, details regarding pricing, privacy, and market availability remain unspecified by the manufacturer.
