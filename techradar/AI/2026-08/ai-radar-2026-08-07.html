Saman Rahbar introduces the Ignition Index (I), a validated scalar metric that measures global workspace dynamics in language models according to Global Workspace Theory (GWT). DGMM generalises mixed effects models to deep learning, providing semi-parametric and flexible modelling of data's mean and correlation structure through fixed and random effects. The model uses an adaptation of variational auto-encoders and Bayesian data augmentation algorithm to accommodate longitudinal outcomes following generic distributions and provide valid inference when data are missing-at-random.

Handling Missing Data in Probabilistic Regression Trees

Probabilistic Regression Trees (PRTrees) can now accommodate missing predictor values directly during tree construction, eliminating the need for prior imputation. Three strategies were proposed to handle missing data: a uniform-probability approach, a partial-observation approach, and a dimension-reduced smoothing approach. The proposed methods frequently outperformed classical regression trees in datasets with substantial missing predictor values.

Beyond Marginal Validity: Finite-Sample Guarantees for Localized Conformal Prediction

Researchers Anton Conrad, Rustam Isaev, Denis Belomestny, Eric Moulines, and Sergey Samsonov provide finite-sample guarantees for localized conformal prediction. Under Hölder regularity of the conditional score CDF and standard density and kernel assumptions, they prove high-probability bounds for the conditional-coverage gap and the length error relative to the oracle. The bounds decompose into an $O(h^\beta)$ localization bias and a calibration term decreasing with calibration size.

Minimax Optimal Early-Stopped Gradient Descent for Gaussian Mixture Classification

Gradient Descent (GD) on logistic loss can diverge in norm while converging in direction to a max-margin interpolating classifier in overparameterized classification, leading to statistically suboptimal implicit bias. Early stopping can overcome this suboptimality by achieving minimax-optimal excess zero-one risk for Gaussian mixture models with fast and continuous decay rates. A new calibration result converts excess logistic risk into excess zero-one risk, handling model misspecification induced by label-flipping noise.

Optimal Rates for Learning with Monotone Adversaries

A monotone adversary observes an i.i.d. labeled sample and appends a finite number of further examples, every one correctly labeled by the target hypothesis. The learner sees a uniform shuffle of the combined sample and is scored on the original distribution. The minimax expected error is O((d/n)log(n/d)) for classes of VC dimension d.

Scalable estimation of VARMA models

Researchers have developed a new estimation framework for Vector Autoregressive Moving-Average (VARMA) models that removes computational barriers, enabling scalability to high-dimensional data.
