Palm Garden AI develops Coherence Guard relational decision layer for human-facing robots

Palm Garden AI's Coherence Guard relational decision layer for human-facing robots evaluates whether proposed actions are relationally appropriate before execution, considering factors like timing, proximity, emotional tone, trust preservation, and socially appropriate action. Developed on the Transwarp Cloud Operating System (TCOS), Coherence Guard complements existing safety systems, supporting auditability, logging, scenario testing, and configurable thresholds. The technology is designed to be flexible, running on edge devices or in the cloud, with a preferred deployment model of local-first for critical functions.

Faraday Future highlights robotics expansion and shipment milestone at Automate 2026

FFAI, Faraday Future's AI subsidiary, is accelerating its expansion into commercial robotics after showcasing its "one brain, multiple forms" platform at Automate 2026. The company expects to ship over 100 units of robots by June and has already surpassed its original target with more than 220 shipments in the first half of the year. FFAI introduced a portfolio of products including humanoid robots, mobile manipulators, and education-focused systems built around an Embodied AI software platform.
