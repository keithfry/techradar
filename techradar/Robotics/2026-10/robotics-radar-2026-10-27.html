WorldOracle Tests Whether World Models Can Reliably Predict Real-World Executions

WorldOracle is a benchmark evaluating action-conditioned world models by comparing their predictions against real-world robot executions. It assesses how accurately models predict object positions and movements during manipulation tasks, noting that arm motion is often predicted well but object states frequently deviate from reality. The evaluation also tests model performance over different time horizons, showing that accuracy often degrades over complete operation sequences. The platform measures prediction errors using metrics like Physical State Correctness and Interaction Spatial Alignment, which score alignment with real outcomes. Developers can participate by submitting predictions from open-weight or closed-source models for iterative improvement.

ROS Discourse General: How much RTK fix do your outdoor robots keep under tree canopy? Field data and a test design

A field test investigated the RTK fix quality for outdoor robots operating under tree canopies. The test compared performance under open sky, under trees with rain, and under trees with network RTK corrections. RAIWAY: A Large-Wheeled Bipedal Robot with Contact-Informed Blind Locomotion

RAIWAY is a large-wheeled bipedal robot featuring 20-inch wheels and a contact-informed control approach for locomotion. The system manages contact-dominated rolling locomotion by distilling multi-contact events into a latent representation from simulation data. The controller infers this representation online using proprioception, eliminating the need for direct contact sensing during operation. Experiments showed RAIWAY successfully traversed diverse challenging terrains, including steep slopes and rocky surfaces. The proposed controller outperformed baselines in both indoor and outdoor testing environments.

arXiv - cs.RO

Bracketing Shows the Way: Adaptive Multi-Exposure Policy as Input for Robust Visual SLAM

Rapid illumination changes can disrupt Visual SLAM by saturating images before exposure adaptation occurs, removing necessary visual correspondences. The proposed method, B-Adapt, acquires adaptive exposure brackets to cover the scene dynamic range while maintaining photometric overlap. BVSLAM then directly uses this multi-exposure sequence as constraints in a factor graph state estimator, bypassing HDR merging. Evaluation showed that combining these methods reduced state estimation error by 78% compared to using standard Automatic Exposure images during tunnel-exit transitions. This demonstrates that bracketing improves visual pose estimation robustness to illumination disturbances.

Interpretable Collision Avoidance and Feasibility Guarantees through an Uncertainty Aware Pipeline

The research proposes a modular perception-prediction-planning pipeline to ensure interpretable collision avoidance and feasibility guarantees for autonomous vehicles. This pipeline propagates uncertainty as structured quantities across modules, decoupling safety guarantees from solver feasibility. It resolves collision probabilities upstream to construct convex safe sets for the planner. Experimental results show the pipeline achieves zero collisions and infeasibility at real-time speeds, outperforming baseline methods. The approach is validated on physical platforms and demonstrates real-time viability.

arXiv - cs.RO

Neuromorphic Decision Dynamics for Deadlock-free Safe Navigation in Congested Environments

Neuromorphic decision dynamics (NDD) is introduced as a decentralized, event-based controller for safe navigation in congested environments. NDD ensures safety, liveness, robustness, and scalability by making safe-and-live deconfliction an emergent property of local agent interactions. Agents modulate speed based on local motion, allowing them to self-organize into conflict-resolving groups. These groups emerge and stabilize when safety-critical conflicts trigger coordinated resolution, guaranteeing collision avoidance without deadlock or livelock. Explicit conditions ensure collision avoidance and traversal-order formation for finite, heterogeneous agent populations.

arXiv - cs.RO

Tiny Recursive Policy: Recursive Latent Refinement for Visuomotor Control

Tiny Recursive Policy (TRP) is a parameter-efficient behavior-cloning policy that incorporates persistent temporal state with recursive latent refinement for visuomotor control. TRP updates high- and low-level latent states repeatedly at each control step using a shared module before generating actions. The method evaluates well on benchmarks like LIBERO-100 and MetaWorld ML45, showing competitive performance in multi-task and few-shot settings. The study indicates that reliance on persistent carry increases with temporal-context demand, and training with carry perturbations enhances robustness. Ablations show that the benefit of the persistent state interacts strongly with the amount of within-step computation.

arXiv - cs.RO

Active Reachability Learning for Humanoid Recoverability Monitoring

The research develops a framework to monitor the recoverability of humanoid policies during deployment to anticipate unrecoverable falls. It addresses the challenge that existing methods lack sufficient training data for the full recoverability envelope, leading to optimistic hallucinations for out-of-distribution inputs. The proposed solution uses a unified framework that alternates between value learning and active exploration, reframing data exploration as a reach-avoid problem. This method systematically discovers unseen states while avoiding confidently unrecoverable regions, supported by combined TD and Monte Carlo objectives. The approach successfully improves monitor coverage and accuracy on benchmarks and transfers to real-world humanoid hardware.

MOTIO: Agentic Skill Engineering for Reality-Grounded Humanoid Loco-Manipulation

MOTIO is an agentic skill-engineering system designed for reality-grounded humanoid loco-manipulation. It synthesizes human action videos, 3D assets, motion references, and robot skills into executable programs. The system uses in-context reasoning to construct staged interactions and refines them via physics execution and human validation. Agent-generated skills supervise distillation into a shared reference policy, enabling task completion without constant expert intervention. This approach successfully prepares long-horizon skills, such as bottle disposal, and reduces physical residual errors.
