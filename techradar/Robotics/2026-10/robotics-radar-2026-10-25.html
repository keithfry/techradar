Eka Robotics raises $150M to scale its vision-force-action model for robots

Eka Robotics raised $150 million in Series A funding to scale its vision-force-action (VFA) model. This model develops an intuition of physics for robots through reinforcement learning, enabling them to generalize across objects, tasks, and environments. The technology allows robots to perform complex actions like screwing in a light bulb by understanding physics rather than mimicking human data. The company's simulation learning approach is reducing the sim-to-real barrier, allowing for vast robot experience generation. Investors supported the funding, recognizing the VFA model's potential for scalable and performant dexterous manipulation.

Unitree develops Rehearsal to improve humanoid robot skills

Unitree Robotics is developing Unitree Rehearsal to enhance humanoid robot skills using AI research, code generation, simulation, and physical testing. This system involves an AI workflow where research papers are analyzed, code is written, and simulations are run before physical trials on robots. Unitree already incorporates AI to collect feedback from physical tests to refine its models. The company forecasts that robots could complete 80% of voice-assigned tasks by 2028, covering factory and household work. The core focus for developers is the feedback loop between simulated tests and physical trials.
