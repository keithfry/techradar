Beyond the Showcase: AGIBOT Expands Embodied AI Portfolio at WAIC 2026

AGIBOT introduced four new embodied AI products at WAIC 2026, expanding its portfolio across commercial, educational, and industrial sectors. The AGIBOT A3 Ultra is a full-size humanoid with 51 active degrees of freedom, while the X2 EDU is an open and modular development platform for research and academia. The G2 Max is a heavy-payload robot designed for material handling and palletizing, and the OmniHand 3 Ultra-M offers a flagship direct-drive dexterous manipulation system featuring 20 active degrees of freedom and vision-based tactile sensing. AGIBOT deployed over 60 robots across WAIC venues to demonstrate scalable capabilities and validate its hardware in real-world scenarios.

Photos: First look at J&J’s Ottava surgical robot

Johnson & Johnson's Ottava surgical robot has four robotic arms integrated into the operating table, taking up 30-50% less space than traditional boom-and-cart systems. The system features a console with a natural posture design to minimize eye and neck strain, and allows surgeons to save personalized settings. Ottava's instruments include a two-in-one needle driver with adjustable modes and patented scissor design for consistent cuts. The robot works within the Polyphonic digital ecosystem for procedure data collection and research, covering various general surgical procedures.
