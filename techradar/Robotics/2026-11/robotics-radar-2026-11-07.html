Industry experts discuss China blocking SoftBank acquisition of ABB Robotics

China blocked SoftBank's acquisition of ABB Robotics due to concerns that training data for AI models could be transferred overseas. This action stems from regulatory worries regarding the transfer of robotics data, which is now viewed as strategically important for physical AI development. Other countries are also taking action to protect sovereign physical AI, with examples including U.S. bans on certain autonomous robots. The issue highlights growing concerns about robotics data, intellectual property, and local manufacturing capabilities in cross-border mergers. Ultimately, the situation reflects broader geopolitical tensions concerning the convergence of automation, data, and artificial intelligence.

Ep#115: Delta-0 (Δ₀): A New Chapter in Humanoid Intelligence

Delta-0 is a humanoid foundation model capable of long-horizon, whole-body locomotion and manipulation using generalist policies trained on whole-body human data. It achieves this by jointly designing the policy and controller, utilizing a delta action space for seamless human intervention during execution. The model demonstrates impressive abilities in everyday tasks like operating a turntable, making the bed, and interacting with objects by coordinating movement and force. The research emphasizes that long-horizon loco-manipulation is challenging due to the coupled control, perception, and dynamic nature of the environment. Key design principles include whole-body control, execution fidelity, and compliance to enable reliable real-world deployment.
