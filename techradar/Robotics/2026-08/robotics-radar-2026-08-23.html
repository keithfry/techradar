Cyborg roaches to the rescue?

Researchers developed a diving suit and oxygen generation system for Madagascar hissing cockroaches to enable underwater and low-oxygen survival. These cyborg insects are guided remotely and can navigate environments like floods, rubble, and drains for up to three hours. They have already demonstrated utility by deploying with infrared cameras during an earthquake to identify signs of life. This capability suggests a greater potential for assisting in underwater search-and-rescue operations. Other biohybrids, such as bionic jellyfish and muscle-powered robots, are also being explored by various research groups.
