Cyborg Roaches Can Stab You With Needles

Australian burrowing cockroaches are being modified into cybernetic "Paraborgs." These robots are remotely controllable to deliver specialized rescue and medical equipment to disaster areas. This technology aims to provide lifesaving aid to victims in emergencies. The project is being conducted by the University of Queensland.

Beyond Mechanical Chatbots: Galbot Unveils RoboGesture to Tackle Real-Time Nonverbal Communication

Galbot developed RoboGesture, a real-time framework to generate semantically aligned and physically safe co-speech gestures for humanoid robots. The system operates in the robot's kinematic space, using a hierarchical aligner and a diffusion transformer with flow matching to generate motion at 120 FPS. To prevent ignoring live speech, the framework incorporates an Anti-Inertia classifier-free guidance masking technique. This work addresses the "modality eclipse" by learning directly in the kinematic space, which avoids prior retargeting bottlenecks and ensures physical safety via an MPC filter. Despite advancements, the end-to-end conversational loop latency is currently limited by the upstream speech processing pipeline.
