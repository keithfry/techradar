XPeng IRON walks off automated line, targets 2026 production

XPeng's IRON humanoid has completed assembly and exited an automated production line, with mass production targeted for the end of 2026. The company claims over 80% of core manufacturing processes are automated, utilizing automotive-grade quality standards. The robot features 76 degrees of freedom and uses proprietary onboard AI chips for autonomous task execution. Funding supports a commercial rollout plan starting with internal deployments before a broader launch in 2027. XPeng expects higher gross margins on humanoid robots due to their technical complexity.

Audi humanoid robot trial targets parts sequencing in Ingolstadt

Audi is testing wheeled humanoid robots at its Ingolstadt plant to pick and sequence vehicle parts. These robots use image recognition with two arms to handle variously shaped and packaged components from containers. The system is trained using real and simulated images to handle variations in parts effectively. The testing aims to validate the reliable performance of the sequencing task in a logistics setting. This project involves collaboration between Audi, Agile Robots, Dassault Systèmes, and NVIDIA to address automation challenges.
