Nuclear-powered spaceships

NASA plans to build Space Reactor-1 Freedom, the first nuclear-reactor-powered interplanetary spacecraft, for an uncrewed mission to Mars by 2029. This propulsion method uses a nuclear reaction to generate electricity for ion propulsion, offering high efficiency for long-duration space travel. Despite past setbacks, NASA is pursuing this technology as a $2 billion bet, focusing on nuclear electric propulsion. The spacecraft concept involves a reactor with a 20-kilowatt capacity and fins for heat radiation. Development is advancing, with the Idaho National Laboratory working on the reactor, and the technology may also pave the way for nuclear power on the Moon.

It’s a bird, it’s a plane … it’s a robo-puffin?

Engineers at MIT and EPFL designed a robot called a flapping-wing aerial-aquatic vehicle (FAAV) capable of transitioning between swimming and flying. This small vehicle uses flexible wings, an electric motor, and a steerable tail to achieve this transition. Experiments determined that medium-sized, flexible wings with specific flapping frequencies and a 70-degree tail angle were optimal for the transition. The research aims to create a drone that can access and sample aquatic regions for oceanographic purposes. Future work will focus on improving wing articulation and testing performance in turbulent conditions.

Opinion: Why the next generation of AMRs won’t need engineered environments

Indoor autonomous mobile robots succeed by operating in environments specifically engineered for them, relying on a foundational assumption of controllable spaces. Outdoor autonomy requires robots to adapt to highly variable, unengineered environments, which presents fundamentally different engineering problems. Real-world outdoor deployment is constrained less by perception and more by the system's ability to manage unpredictable transitions and maintain reliability across diverse conditions. Successful outdoor robots must incorporate human coexistence from the start, prioritizing behavioral predictability over mere technical capability. The next decade of AMR development will be defined by platforms designed for unstructured environments, not just those that can operate within engineered settings.

Why Your Robot’s Power Supply is the Real Bottleneck

Power supply issues are often the root cause of robotics failures, despite teams focusing on software and perception. Voltage sags and electrical noise from motors and drivers frequently cause performance problems that software alone cannot fix. Understanding power architecture is crucial because it dictates the reliability of sensors and compute modules. Datasheets must be read defensively, focusing on load response, voltage dropout, and thermal derating rather than just efficiency. Reliable robot design requires treating the power stage as a first-class design problem to ensure system stability.

DexGraspFlow: Topology-Aware SE(3) Flow Matching for Cross-Embodiment Dexterous Grasp Generation

DexGraspFlow is a framework that uses topology-aware SE(3) flow matching to generate dexterous grasps across different embodied systems. It integrates geometric relations and kinematic topology to address the challenges of cross-embodiment grasp generation. The model employs dual-stream Diffusion Transformers to model link-link and link-object coordination, incorporating attention biases from kinematic-tree distances. The framework learns velocity fields to generate link poses, which are then inverted to recover joint configurations. Experiments show high success rates and reduced latency compared to state-of-the-art methods, demonstrating zero-shot adaptability to variations.

arXiv - cs.RO

Replan for Error Recovery: Training Embodied VLMs through Verifiable Interaction with Action-Grouped Turn-GSPO

Embodied VLMs need replanning for error recovery when execution mistakes occur during complex manipulation tasks. The proposed method introduces the Embodied Replanning for Recovery Environment (ERR-Env) to provide verifiable feedback on task progress and errors. Action-Grouped Turn-GSPO (AGT-GSPO) is an online reinforcement learning technique that uses turn-level rewards and error states to train an embodied VLM. This method is applied to train an ERR-VLM on an ERR-Bench benchmark featuring nominal, intermediate, and error states. Evaluation shows that AGT-GSPO significantly improves the VLM's success rate for error-free completion and recovery.

Embodied Playground: A Long-Horizon Agentic Robotics Benchmark for Physical, Strategic, and Spatial Reasoning

Embodied Playground is a benchmark designed to evaluate foundation-model agents' ability to coordinate physical, strategic, and spatial reasoning over long task horizons. Agents must track progress, adhere to constraints, and adjust plans based on execution feedback during these tasks. The playground encompasses 410 environments covering manipulation, puzzles, and household activities with varying complexity. It includes curated subsets like EP-100 and EP-Easy/Hard for structured evaluation. The framework provides a unified method to study sustained reasoning and action in embodied robotics.

General-purpose multimodal models show strong spatial perception but require stronger grounding for physical tasks. The research develops a unified data pipeline to create verifiable grounding supervision by combining robot recordings with visual data. Scaling verifiable grounding supervision effectively strengthens the spatial perception of foundation models for embodied operations.

arXiv - cs.RO

Towards Interaction State Recognition in Sensorized Gecko-Inspired Adhesive Interfaces for Orbital Servicing

Reliable contact establishment is a challenge for autonomous orbital servicing using gecko-inspired dry adhesives. This paper introduces a framework to estimate the contact state of these interfaces using embedded capacitive sensing during the adhesion lifecycle. Five interaction states (idle, proximity, contact, engaged, and disengaged) are classified using sensor data and evaluated with six machine learning models. The Random Forest model achieved 95.65% offline accuracy, improving to 93.14% online accuracy when combined with a rule-based state machine. Incorporating physics-consistent state constraints enhances robustness for real-time interaction-aware capture in space missions.

Visual Task Modeling and Automated Planning for Humanoid Robots in Education of Individuals with Autism Spectrum Disorder

A novel method is presented for enabling semi-autonomous robots to recognize task goals and plan actions for educational tasks. This research focuses on "Shoebox Tasks," which are visually defined activities used for educating individuals with Autism Spectrum Disorder (ASD). A solver utilizing a vision-based Large Language Model converts task images into a Planning Domain Definition Language (PDDL) representation and generates action plans. A robotic interface allows a robot, like a Nao robot, to autonomously execute these tasks and provide assistance through demonstrations or commands. The system aims to enhance robot-assisted learning opportunities and foster interest in robotics for students with ASD.

arXiv - cs.RO

Learning to Be Different: Component Specialization for Compositional Diffusion Policies

Diffusion policies with linearly composed scores are used for multitasking robotic learning through separate factor combination. Current methods fail to encourage component specialization, leading to uninterpretable denoisers that average identical predictions. The authors propose a drop-in score-decorrelation auxiliary loss, based on projective composition theory, to encourage specialization. This loss penalizes overlap between component noise predictions, promoting specialization without architectural changes. Empirical results show this loss reduces double-counting and improves task decomposition while maintaining multitask performance.

arXiv - cs.RO

EmbodyHOI: Embodiment-Aware Generation of Hand-Object Interactions for Humanoid Dexterous Manipulation

EmbodyHOI generates hand-object interactions for humanoid dexterous manipulation by incorporating embodiment-aware constraints into the trajectory generation process. It achieves this by running a differentiable inverse-kinematics solver within the sampling loop of a flow-matching trajectory generator. Gradients from this embodiment-aware solver guide the HOI sampler, jointly optimizing the humanoid's position relative to the object. This method significantly reduces the retargeted wrist error and improves task success rates compared to other embodiment bridging techniques. EmbodyHOI demonstrates superior performance in bridging the embodiment gap for robotic manipulation tasks.

arXiv - cs.RO

XoNav: An Exoskeleton as a Personalized Haptic Interface for Assisted Navigation

XoNav is a four-actuator hip exoskeleton designed to provide personalized haptic guidance for assisted navigation. It delivers advance cues, braking cues, and steering cues directly at the hip joints based on gait-state classification from IMUs. A bilateral LSTM classifier identifies gait states, allowing cues to be delivered during walking while suppressed during standing. Bayesian optimization personalizes braking for minimum resistance and steering for maximum comfort while ensuring safety. Experimental trials showed participants successfully navigated blindfolded paths with improved speed and accuracy.
