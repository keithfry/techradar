Tesla Reportedly Begins New Optimus Supplier Audits in China

Chinese media reported that Tesla initiated new supplier audits for Optimus components in Ningbo on September 17, with component orders already placed. These audits focused on manufacturing consistency and equipment commissioning as preparation for robot production. Supplier representatives confirmed cooperation with Tesla on related robotics projects but did not explicitly confirm the latest audit visits. These developments suggest progress in the supply chain alongside factory construction, though no new production timetable was established. The audits represent a preparatory step toward realizing potential robot output.
