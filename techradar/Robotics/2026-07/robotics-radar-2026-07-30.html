Trends and outlook for actuators: The muscles behind humanoid motion

Humanoid robotics is transitioning to structured pilot deployments and early commercial adoption, driven by a 47% CAGR for humanoid unit sales over the next decade. Actuators are critical components responsible for motion, with electric actuators being the most popular among current robots, accounting for around 56% of a humanoid's weight. Soft actuators, using materials like electroactive polymers and shape memory alloys, are emerging as potential alternatives, but face challenges such as poor adaptability to unstructured environments. IDTechEx reports that material innovations will be key to developing next-generation humanoid hardware, with 10-year demand forecasts for key materials forecasted.

Towards Trustworthy Embodied Intelligence: A Systems Framework and Graded Trustworthiness Levels

A systems framework for trustworthy embodied intelligence integrates four interdependent layers: the model layer, system layer, evidence layer, and deployment layer. The model layer generates task-competent action proposals with calibrated uncertainty, while the system layer realizes authorized actions dependably through integrated sensing, computation, control, and safeguards. The hierarchy of trustworthiness levels grades bounded deployment claims across task capability, safety, system assurance, operational governance, and supporting evidence, providing a basis for comparative evaluation, research prioritization, and future standardization.

Embodied Agents Take Control: Minimal-Interface Zero-Shot Agents Rival Industrial-Scale Policies in Vision-and-Language Navigation

Researchers have developed minimal-interface zero-shot agents, also known as agentic embodied control, that can rival industrial-scale policies in vision-and-language navigation. These agents sustain a long decision-making loop using only a monocular RGB camera and discrete actions, achieving 70.7% success in zero-shot navigation tasks. When a trained waypoint tool is exposed alongside primitives, the hybrid fable-5 agent reaches 76.7% success with half the environment steps and less than one quarter of the wall time of the maximum-effort primitive run.

arXiv - cs.RO

Multi-Objective Compliance-Integrated Coevolution For Simulated And Real-World Deployment Of Multi-Robot Marine Autonomy

A multi-objective framework, Marine Multi-Objective Compliance-Integrated Coevolution (MMOCIC), is introduced to balance team progress with norm violations in real-world deployments of learning-based coordination for collaborative robots. The framework blends coevolved behaviors with compliance behaviors and demonstrates high team performance while avoiding collisions on a swimmer rescue mission with up to 8 vehicles in hardware deployment, and 12 vehicles in simulation. The key contribution is the decoupling of learning from compliance since operational norms are prescribed rather than discovered. MMOCIC achieves this by processing sparse feedback signals to generate coordinated behaviors.

MoMo: Dial Motion Mode in Robot Manipulation with Spatiotemporal Action Tokenization

MoMo, a two-stage imitation-learning framework, uses a spatiotemporal action tokenizer and a behavior-cloning transformer to control motion modes in robot manipulation tasks. Across six real-robot tasks, varying MoMo's motion mode produces steady, dynamic, and intermediate behaviors that can be distinguished by human raters. These results demonstrate compositional generalization of motion modes across unseen task-mode combinations, showing that motion mode can be reused across tasks.

arXiv - cs.RO

Reeling It In: Flexible Needle Pick Up via Thread Manipulation for Autonomous Suturing

Researchers have developed a new framework for flexible needle pick up via thread manipulation to enable autonomous suturing, avoiding direct tool-tissue contact and enabling pickup even when the needle is occluded or inaccessible. The framework uses a suture thread as an assistive tool, reconstructing thread and tissue configuration, selecting safe grasp points, lifting threads stably, and following threads bimanually until securing needle grasping. This work evaluates the proposed framework on a da Vinci Research Kit under various real-world conditions, demonstrating robust performance even with challenging thread configurations or non-approachable needles.

arXiv - cs.RO

Self-Adaptive Learning and Model Predictive Control for Tracking Unknown Dynamics with No Regret

A self-adaptive online learning method for tracking unknown target dynamics with no regret is proposed. The method simultaneously learns multiple predictors from scratch via self-supervised, one-shot, and computationally efficient learning, and adaptively selects the best one to match the observed target behavior. In the absence of error and switching, the method asymptotically matches the optimal non-causal control policy, while in the presence of errors and switching, it degrades gracefully with a regret proportional to the average learning error and switching times.

Reinforcement Learning on Cost-Constrained Quadrupedal Hardware

Researchers have developed a method to improve locomotion in cost-constrained quadrupedal hardware using reinforcement learning and a biologically inspired approach. By incorporating a forward model of the average actuator delay into a time-aware neural network, they achieve robust locomotion despite a 50ms transport delay. The proposed method also leads to the discovery of a central pattern generator (CPG) that mirrors the CPGs found in vertebrates, enabling the quadruped to adapt to latency perturbations. This approach may be a general strategy for cost-constrained locomotion.

RLMM-Flow: A Flow-based Mobile Manipulation Framework with Latent-Space Reinforcement Learning

RLMM-Flow combines expert flow-policy pretraining with latent-space reinforcement learning post-training to improve mobile manipulation framework quality. A flow policy captures a multimodal whole-body motion prior from expert demonstrations, and a latent steering network steers its initial noise toward higher-value action chunks. Joint training of the latent critic and actor improves task success, collision avoidance, and trajectory quality over imitation-only flow policies and existing reinforcement learning post-training baselines.

Speech2Grasp: Data-Efficient Transfer of Text-Conditioned Grasp Detection to Speech in Humanoid Robots

Researchers developed Speech2Grasp, a framework to transfer text-conditioned grasp detection to speech in humanoid robots, improving upon existing approaches. Using ALBEF as a case study, they found that adapting the model to speech using a lightweight MLP-based projector preserves semantic discrimination and robustness. The real-world experiments showed that Speech2Grasp outperforms traditional cascaded ASR-based pipelines while reducing inference latency.
