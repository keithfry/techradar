Melotte reduces weekly 3D printing quality data handling from three hours to 20 minutes with amsight

Melotte implemented amsight to streamline the management of additive manufacturing quality data. The system connects build, CT, sensor, and maintenance data, creating a traceable quality backbone for demanding industrial markets.

Zipline makes drone deliveries quieter with real-time acoustic flight optimization

Zipline has implemented sound-optimized dynamic flying using onboard sound modeling to achieve quieter drone deliveries. This optimization has resulted in a 68 percent reduction in overall sound exposure per flight and 24 percent quieter flights at peak volume compared to the 2025 fleet. The drones use real-time wind and altitude data to dynamically adjust their transitions to on-wing flight, mimicking natural flight behaviors. This method also reduces flight times by an average of 53 seconds and decreases energy consumption by 21 percent per flight. This technology allows for even quieter flights and enables future higher-altitude operations.

Welcome, Atlas on Wheels!

Atlas on Wheels is a wheeled humanoid research prototype from Boston Dynamics designed to study electric actuator performance through unusual locomotion. It utilizes scooter wheels attached to existing ankle motors to investigate demanding motor behavior while retaining an existing humanoid upper body. The platform possesses capabilities for obstacle avoidance, jumping and landing, and sorting goods, though its overall skill score is low. Standing 190 cm tall and weighing 90 kilograms, it is primarily a research tool focusing on the relationship between mobile navigation, actuator performance, and handling. This prototype represents an experimental configuration of the Atlas robot developed to inform its final design.
