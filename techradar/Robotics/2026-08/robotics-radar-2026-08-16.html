San Mateo County Moves to Regulate Humanoid Robots, Threatening Teleoperation Business Models

The San Mateo County Board of Supervisors has unanimously passed a resolution directing the creation of a comprehensive permitting process for commercial humanoid robots. The proposed ordinance requires humanoid robots to be "supervised at all times by a trained, on-site human" and mandates economic impact assessments and potential retraining fees for displaced workers. Annual cost-recovery fees will fund specialized HazMat equipment for local first responders to mitigate thermal runaway fire risks, and strict hardware vetting will be required to ensure a clean manufacturer safety record.

Figure CEO Brett Adcock Claims Humanoids Are Already Delivering ROI Amid Severe Labor Shortages

Figure AI's CEO, Brett Adcock, asserts that the primary challenge in the humanoid sector is achieving high-level onboard AI, not hardware mass manufacturing. Early commercial partners are already achieving a return on investment due to severe labor shortages and annual turnover rates exceeding 100% in some sectors. Figure's focus has shifted from proving hardware works to solving critical labor shortages for enterprise clients.

LG to Unveil Next-Gen Humanoid Built on NVIDIA Isaac GR00T in Early 2027

LG is developing a next-generation humanoid robot powered by NVIDIA's Isaac GR00T foundation model and Jetson Thor compute platform, targeting a Q1 2027 public unveiling. The robot will utilize LG's internal supply chain resources, leveraging actuators, sensors, and batteries developed in-house. LG plans to deploy its CLOiD wheel-based robots at its Tennessee washing machine manufacturing plant to gather real-world training data, and will build an 80 MW AI factory in Cheonan, South Korea, by 2028 to process physical AI datasets.
