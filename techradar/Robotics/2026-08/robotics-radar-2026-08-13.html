No Fingers, No Problem: X Square Robot’s 1,816-Parcel-Per-Hour Livestream Challenges Figure

X Square Robot completed a one-hour live logistics challenge, sorting 1,816 parcels with a success rate of over 98%. The dual-arm robotic system used purpose-built grippers instead of complex five-finger dexterous hands, prioritizing high-speed and cost-effective throughput. The company emphasized its commercial practicality in hardware choices, focusing on real-world deployment rather than marketing flash. This challenge followed similar formats by Figure AI and AGIBOT, showcasing the technical hurdle of using vision-based AI foundation models to process raw camera pixels and generate real-time motor torque for manipulation in unpredictable environments.

The Physical Bottleneck: Why Actuator Technology is the Next Frontier for Humanoid Robots

Humanoid robotics has reached a critical point where cognitive AI capabilities are outpacing hardware advancements. Traditional industrial robotic joints are insufficient for dynamic motion required in unpredictable environments. Quasi-Direct Drive (QDD) architectures provide low impedance, backdrivability, and precise force interaction for next-generation humanoid platforms. Integrated actuator solutions, such as those by CubeMars, combine motors, drivers, and encoders into compact systems to accelerate reliable real-world deployment.

Adaptation of Generalist Robot Policies with Minimal Data

Researchers Shreyas Kowshik and his team developed MiDAS, a simple offline-to-online RL recipe that adapts generalist robot policies with minimal data. The approach uses behavior cloning on single or few demonstrations to anchor a pre-trained policy to the target task, followed by value-based online RL improvements through autonomous interaction. With as little as one demonstration, MiDAS recovers strong task performance across various robot platforms and outperforms baselines, demonstrating reliable robot policy adaptation from minimal human guidance.

Top-down Traffic Scenario Generation via Joint Initial-Goal Diffusion and Trajectory Infilling

Researchers propose TrafficDiffuser, a top-down traffic scenario generation framework that jointly models initial and goal state pairs to generate high-level scenarios. The framework improves upon existing methods by making initial states better interpretable and reducing trajectory generation into an infilling problem. It outperforms the next-best approach for agent initialization, capturing real-world distributions on the Argoverse 2 motion prediction dataset with a reduction in speed distribution distance by 55.3% and off-road rate by 2.8%.

From Self-Normal-Positioning to Omni-Directional Tracking: Real-Time Surface Modeling Enabled Probe Tilt Control for Robotic Ultrasound Imaging

Robotic ultrasound imaging systems can improve acquisition consistency, but most existing systems only focus on normal positioning. A new framework integrates RGB-D perception, local-surface modeling, and task-space orientation control to enable omni-directional probe-orientation control. The system recovered a non-normal tilt angle of up to 44.39 deg relative to the surface normal and acquired desired heart chamber views in phantom and in-vivo experiments, with a mean angular tracking error of 1.06 ± 0.66 deg.

In a user study, participants push an intelligent powered wheelchair under three conditions: no assistance, conventional variability-reducing assistance, and variability-preserving assistance. Researchers have introduced a "neuro-symbolic safety guard" to improve the safety and reliability of end-to-end autonomous driving agents. The guard, attached to the final command interface of an already-trained agent, checks explicit safety rules before executing commands. arXiv - cs.RO

Koopman Representation of Nonlinear Virtual Environments in Kinesthetic Haptic Systems

Researchers Yanting Zhou, Jozsef Kövecses, and James Richard Forbes propose using the Koopman operator to represent nonlinear virtual environments (VEs) in kinesthetic haptic systems. Simulation and experimental results show that this method provides an effective representation of a Duffing-oscillator VE's nonlinear dynamics. The proposed method is more robust than traditional methods, as it uses a linear combination of lifted states to represent nonlinearity. A closed-loop stability analysis leveraging the Koopman representation confirms the overall haptic system's stability.

arXiv - cs.RO

RoadWeaver: Large-Scale Lane-Level HD Map Generation from Scratch for Autonomous Driving Simulation

RoadWeaver, a coarse-to-fine framework, generates large-scale HD maps from scratch for autonomous driving simulation. It first synthesizes global road layout, expands it into connected network, and constructs lane-level geometry with topologically consistent connectivity. Experimental results show 99.8% reachability, 10.7% dead-end ratio, and 0.24m endpoint alignment error, reducing SOTA generation methods' errors by 94.4%. The generated maps can be deployed in driving simulators, providing scalable simulation environments for future closed-loop evaluation of autonomous driving systems.
