GAMEGO: Training Game-Dev Agents with Synthetic Trajectories Anchored in Real-World Assets

GameGo is a framework designed to train game-development agents using synthetic trajectories anchored in real-world assets. The framework utilizes GameGoData, comprising 55,060 development trajectories across various game dimensions, and GameGoBench for evaluation. Training a model on this data results in agents that perform comparably to frontier models on gamedev benchmarks.

FluidPD: In-Place Elasticity for SLO-Aware Prefill-Decode Disaggregated LLM Serving

Prefill-decode disaggregation requires managing dynamic imbalances in resource allocation for LLM serving. FluidPD introduces in-place elasticity to maintain Service Level Objectives (SLOs) by adjusting resource distribution without adding hardware. It uses FluidToken to handle transient imbalances by shifting work between prefill and decode workers when slack exists. FluidRole manages sustained imbalances by reassigning running workers between the two roles without restarting the model. This approach improved SLO attainment by up to 94.6% over static methods on production workloads.

Anchor Divergence for Semantic Geometry in Contrastive Learning

Contrastive representations naturally involve a family of geometries that can be specialized based on semantic context, unlike fixed cosine similarity. The paper proposes using contrastive learning, exponential families, and information geometry to link probability distributions over anchors to Bregman geometries. This connection allows for defining "Anchor Divergences" to specify context-specific semantic geometries on learned representations. Modeling the anchor distribution under this framework effectively specifies context-dependent semantic similarity. Experiments demonstrate that anchor divergences provide an efficient method for specifying this context-specific similarity.

Transferability and operational reliability of a Prithvi crop classification foundation model under phenological and geographic shift across three continents

Fine-tuned geospatial foundation models show reduced accuracy when applied across different continents due to phenological and geographic shifts. Evaluation across 12 countries on three continents revealed a significant drop in overall accuracy from 0.65 in the US to 0.40 in Europe. Performance collapse was linked to misalignment between observation windows and local crop phenology rather than pure spatial transfer. Consequently, these models are only reliably transferable where observation windows align with local growing seasons.

KVFetch: Temporal Prefetching for the Missing Half of KV Cache Compression

KV cache compression is necessary for efficient LLM inference as context windows grow. Current compression methods only consider content relevance for eviction, ignoring sequential positional information. KVFetch introduces a training-free method to temporally prefetch positional successors for score-based compressors. This approach recovers verbatim copying and improves performance on tasks requiring sequential access.

The research investigates the resource requirements—state, context, or dynamical criticality—needed for accurate forecasting in sequence models, focusing on long-range temporal dependence. Experimental results confirm these theoretical state and stability predictions across various model types.

Numerical experiments validate the approach for various energy minimization problems.

The Impact of Likelihood Tempering on the Limiting Predictive Moments of Variational Bayesian Linear Neural Networks

Gaussian mean-field variational inference in wide Bayesian neural networks suffers from prior dominance due to the KL regularization term. Tempering the likelihood by raising it to the power $1/T$ scales the KL term by $T$, allowing for a balance between likelihood and regularization. The paper investigates the relationship between the tempering parameter $T$ and network width $M$ for single-hidden-layer linear networks. Limiting predictive moments exhibit phase transitions depending on the scaling $T = \tau/M^{c}$. These transitions reveal how the predictive expectation and variance relate to the infinite-width limit, the Neural Network Gaussian Process (NNGP) posterior.
