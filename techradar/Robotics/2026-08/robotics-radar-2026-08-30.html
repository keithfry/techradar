From AlphaGo to AstraTennis: Galbot Humanoids Complete 100+ Consecutive Rallies in Live Tennis Match

Galbot humanoids completed over 100 consecutive rallies in a live tennis match, branded as "AstraTennis," at the second World Humanoid Robot Games. The demonstration showcased the robots executing various tennis skills, including serves, rallies, and dynamic balance recovery in real time. This achievement validated Galbot's LATENT framework and the capabilities of their AstraBrain architecture for dynamic athletic control. The tennis demonstration served as a demanding physical stress test for embodied AI, requiring real-time perception and coordination. This event represents a significant step in applying agile, reactive intelligence to complex physical tasks.
