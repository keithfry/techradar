Ep#113: MolmoBOT: Large-Scale Simulation Enables Zero-Shot Manipulation

MolmoBOT uses large-scale simulation to generate diverse data, aiming to enable zero-shot manipulation policies without requiring real-world robot data. The system includes the MolmoBot-Engine for procedural data generation and MolmoBot-Data, a dataset of 1.7 million expert trajectories. Three policy classes, including MolmoBot, MolmoBot-SPOC, and MolmoBot-Pi0, were trained on this synthetic data. These policies achieved 79.2% success rates in real-world pick-and-place tasks with zero real-world fine-tuning. The work demonstrates that extensive simulated data can facilitate effective sim-to-real transfer.

Using AI Agents to Teach Robots Like Microduck to Walk in Simulation

AI agents like Hermes and Nemotron plan an obstacle course, which is then used to create 3D assets via Astra for simulation. These assets are assembled in NVIDIA Isaac Sim, incorporating Newton for physics modeling and the Omniverse libraries for rendering and sensor simulation. A perception and locomotion policy is then trained at scale in NVIDIA Isaac Lab using parallel environments and observations. The trained policy is validated in Isaac Sim before being prepared for sim-to-real transfer on physical robots.

Generative Bionics plans data factory for GENE.01 humanoid

Generative Bionics plans to build a 5,000 square meter humanoid training facility in Italy by the fourth quarter of 2027. This Physical AI Data Factory will reproduce industrial environments to train and validate the GENE.01 humanoid before customer deployment. The facility will collect physical interaction data, including vision, motion, and multimodal skin signals, to train world action models. The company will create digital twins for each physical environment and robot configuration to simulate thousands of variations. AMD's Helios will provide the computing foundation for processing this physical and synthetic data.
