ROS Discourse General: Properly close windows processes, discussion about implementation

Windows does not have a proper way to handle SIGINT, causing ungraceful shutdowns. The issue is with using `signal.SIGTERM` instead of `signal.CTRL_C_EVENT` on Windows, which is the correct signal to send for proper termination. A developer attempted to implement this change but encountered issues, including increased failures in CI runs and failed pull requests.

Protection Levels for Vision-Based Pose Estimation

A new algorithm for computing protection levels in vision-based pose estimation is presented, which provides probabilistic bounds on pose error under undetected faults. The algorithm covers all six degrees of freedom of the aircraft pose directly and analyzes the effect of measurement redundancy, pixel-level prediction uncertainty, and runway distance on protection levels. The results demonstrate tradeoffs in the protection levels on an illustrative runway example.

The Impact of Operational-Data Fidelity when Assessing Safety-Critical Autonomous-Vehicle Software

Insufficiently detailed operational data can undermine safety claims in autonomous vehicle software assessments, despite conservative analysis. This limitation can lead to dangerously optimistic reliability claims. The lack of detail can result from incomplete or inconsistent data, which can be problematic for assessing the robustness of reliability claims. Conservative Bayesian inference techniques are extended to investigate the impact of data fidelity on assessments.

arXiv - cs.RO

Navigating the Proximity-Safety Balance: Constraint Decomposition for Human Following in Pedestrian Crowds

Researchers have developed a method for human-following in pedestrian crowds, which navigates the proximity-safety balance by decomposing the task into sparse reward and independent cost constraints. This approach allows for explicit control over the trade-off between staying close to the target and navigating safely among surrounding pedestrians and obstacles. The method also incorporates prediction uncertainty of human motions to enhance safety under unpredictable conditions. Extensive experiments demonstrate its effectiveness in both in-distribution and out-of-distribution settings.

arXiv - cs.RO

Whole-Body Planning for Humanoids Navigating Confined Spaces via Self-Collision Avoidance References

Whole-body planning for humanoids navigating confined spaces requires integrating self-collision avoidance into a reachability-constrained formulation. A three-stage framework formulates kinematic path planning directly over kinematically reachable rigid-body volumes, synthesizing volume-informed guides that guide a full-order trajectory optimizer over long horizons. This approach achieves restricted confinement ratios (C_r < 1.5) and generates feasible trajectories across complex foot and hand contacts, while a learned policy successfully tracks these plans under domain randomization.

arXiv - cs.RO

FACT: Failure-Aware Causal Training for World-Action Models

FACT, a causal World-Action Model, predicts future video and task progress conditioned on the executed action. This allows failure rollouts to supervise action consequences, turning bad actions into valid future targets rather than being discarded. Extensive experiments show that FACT outperforms existing baselines, improves with failure data, and reduces success-biased future hallucination under bad actions.

arXiv - cs.RO

A Neural Network Based Teleoperation for Remote Controlled Vehicles

A neural network-based teleoperation framework is proposed to address technical bottlenecks in direct vehicle control, including communication latency and uncertainty compensation. The system integrates the Wave Variable approach for passive stability under stochastic delays and an adaptive Radial Basis Function Network (RBFN) for active compensation of vehicle-specific uncertainties. Simulation benchmarks demonstrate that the RBFN achieves superior robustness against unmodeled disturbances while requiring orders of magnitude less execution time than other control methods. Hardware-in-the-loop experiments validate the system's practical feasibility, safety, and robust trajectory tracking under physical road uncertainties.

arXiv - cs.RO

Real-World Cooperative Bimanual Dexterous Grasp of Large Objects from Single-View Observations

Researchers developed a real-world cooperative bimanual grasping framework that enables the synthesis of executable bimanual grasps from single-view inputs, reducing dependence on complete 3D object models. The approach includes a multimodal dataset, a Denoising Diffusion Probabilistic Model (DDPM)-based module that generates joint-level grasp configurations, and an execution strategy that integrates motion planning with online grasp refinement. Experiments demonstrated high success rates across unseen objects with varying geometries and poses using a dual-arm robot.

arXiv - cs.RO

Hip Energized Monopedal Hopping

Researchers have developed a new stepping strategy for monopeds, utilizing reaction torques to counteract energetic losses from damping and increase pitch stabilization torque. This results in a user-specified balance between steady-state fore-aft speed and apex height. The strategy has been validated through simulation studies on a generic 5-link biped and physical experiments on the Penn Jerboa, achieving speeds of up to 1.77 m/s (8.85 leg lengths/s).

arXiv - cs.RO

PBD-AG: Persistent Baseline-Delta Active Graphs with Uncertainty-Aware Inspection for Long-Horizon Service Robots

PBD-AG presents a persistent baseline-delta active graph framework for long-horizon service robots that autonomously builds structural baseline and inspects fixtures to ground hierarchical object beliefs. The framework maintains reliability-weighted object states over geometry, semantics, identity, existence, and support relations. Simulation experiments show higher aggregate coarse-fixture F1 scores than capability-matched controls, as well as stronger identity continuity and event recall. PBD-AG integrates with onboard sensing, providing a traceable world model for long-horizon robotic applications.

Lost in Reconstruction: Aligning Action Representations with Language in Vision-Language-Action Models

Researchers introduced SALT, a Semantically ALigned action Tokenizer, to improve language-conditioned control in vision-language-action models by aligning action representations with language. This approach enhances verb-specialized codes while maintaining reconstruction fidelity, leading to 71.9% average success in SimplerEnv, compared to 42.7% and 31.2% for other methods.
