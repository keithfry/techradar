Difference-in-Differences vs Lagged-Outcome Regression

Two methods for estimating causal effects in product analytics are Lagged-outcome regression and Difference-in-Differences (DiD). Lagged-outcome regression assumes unconfoundedness, while DiD assumes parallel trends. Both estimators can be implemented using doubly robust machinery, such as DoubleML. The choice between them depends on the identifying assumption and diagnostic checks on the pre-period trend. The pre-trend check, comparing the post-period to the pre-period, helps decide which assumption is more likely to hold.
